Title: Mechanical Keyboard Review
Date: 2026-08-16 15:26
Category: Review

Following is a review of my current mechanical keyboard.

The feel of the keys is very good, providing the satisfying tactile feedback
mechanical keyboards are known for. The typing angle is reasonably comfortable,
making it easy to use for long periods without strain. Overall, it’s a nice
keyboard with decent design and functionality.

I specifically chose the [Adesso AKB-670UB](https://adesso.com/shop/solutions/corporate-solutions/akb-670ub/)
with USB cable after being unhappy with several Logitech membrane
keyboards (both wired and wireless). I use this keyboard with my
daily-driver (a Framework Ryzen 7 laptop) in my home office.

I would definitely by this one again.
